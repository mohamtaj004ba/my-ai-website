Cookie Policy

Last reviewed: October 5, 2026

This page explains cookies, local storage, session storage, and your choices on CallerCore. These technologies store small amounts of information in a browser. Local storage can remain after a tab closes; session storage generally belongs to the current tab session. A cookie can be sent back to the service that set it.

Essential and optional storage

Essential storage supports a service you request, such as remaining signed in or remembering your privacy choice. Optional analytics measures visits and interactions. You can choose Essential only and continue to browse, use contact forms, and request assistance. Optional analytics begins only after you choose Allow analytics.

What optional analytics measures

With permission, the first-party measurement layer can receive page views, referring sources, campaign parameters, device category, active page time, link or button interactions, selected plans, form starts and submissions, form abandonment, and assistant activity events. It does not record ordinary form-field values as you type. Information intentionally submitted through a contact or signup form is processed to handle the request separately.

The hosting environment may supply approximate city, region, or country information for analytics and may process network information to deliver and protect requests. Turning off optional measurement does not disable technical logs, security processing, or data necessary to operate a requested service.

Changing your choice

Select Cookie settings at the bottom left of a public page. Essential only disables optional analytics and removes cc_vid and cc_sid from that browser. Allow analytics enables first-party measurement. The choice applies to that browser and origin; another device, browser, or preview domain may require a separate choice.

Where a browser sends Global Privacy Control, this website treats it as a request to keep optional analytics disabled. We do not use the absence of that signal as permission. A changed or expired choice can cause the prompt to appear again.

Deleting browser data

Your browser’s privacy or site-data settings can delete cookies and browser storage for CallerCore. This may sign you out and reset preferences. Blocking all storage can affect authentication or prevent your preference from being remembered. Clearing browser data does not automatically delete information already held on a server; contact support for an appropriate privacy request.

External providers and payments

External pages and services, including Stripe payment flows and connected services, can use their own cookies or similar technologies under their own notices. This prompt controls CallerCore’s optional website analytics, not another provider’s settings. Review the destination’s policy when using an external service.

Dashboard preferences and other local state

Authenticated dashboard features can remember presentation preferences such as call-log sorting, grouping, and display density. These preferences support the interface you use rather than the optional public-website measurement layer. Their exact browser keys can vary by feature. Clear site data to reset locally saved settings.

Updates and questions

We may update this inventory when storage or features change. Read it together with the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. Contact support@callercore.com with questions about storage or privacy choices.
