# OpenAI engineering financial policy

The owner explicitly removed CallerCore internal OpenAI dollar boundaries on October 22, 2026. This supersedes dollar admission ceilings and reservation-headroom requirements for the existing engineering development workflow. OpenAI project billing is the authoritative record of billed API charges. It is separate from ChatGPT/Codex subscription usage.

Internal estimates, token receipts, retained reservations, and unresolved counting fees remain intact for comparison. No historical amount is erased, marked refunded, or represented as invoice-reconciled. Historical dollar fields in signed approvals and sequence records remain unchanged identity/evidence metadata; they do not impose a current engineering dollar admission ceiling. A negative historical remainder is not an execution denial under the monitor-only policy.

There is no internal dollar cutoff or assurance of a hard provider billing cap. Billing can lag; local token calculations are estimates and unknown charges remain unknown. The application does not claim automatic billing synchronization or new provider billing permissions. Read existing authorized project billing periodically and preserve the observation date, scope and reconciliation limitations.

Owner/workspace/task/release binding, exact allowed model, request/token/time limits, serial execution, no automatic provider retry, revocation, cancellation, cleanup and emergency stop remain enforced. The later owner development policy also removes the historical ten-attempt ceiling for current v3 sequences; original counts remain immutable historical metadata. This financial policy grants no execution, publication, customer-data, credential or independent-acceptance authority. Historical consumed/revoked approvals remain unusable. New sandbox/browser infrastructure purchases, live financial transactions and Production remain outside this OpenAI policy.

The implementation removes financial admission checks consistently across controller, configuration admission, cumulative sequence, stage admission, response accounting, local recovery selection, legacy store/organization/pilot/Chief/hybrid paths and dormant recovery/autonomy planners. These callers use the existing shared financial policy and estimate validator; valid usage may exceed historical estimates while numeric overflow and forged receipts remain rejected. It preserves the existing ledger and gateway. Historical approval identities and contract fixtures are preserved. Applying the shared financial policy to old code paths does not reactivate consumed or revoked approvals.
