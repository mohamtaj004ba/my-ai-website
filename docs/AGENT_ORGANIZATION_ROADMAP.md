# CallerCore internal agent organization roadmap

Recovery foundations now exist as disabled offline policy/simulation only (AGENT_OPERATIONS_RECOVERY.md). Future Chief remediation may route to a different specialist or Engineering Auditor within separately enforced grants; independent QA remains mandatory, original failed evidence stays immutable, and new candidate acceptance requires fresh bindings. No recovery executor, department lead, Auditor model, paid retry or write capability is activated. Detailed final reporting is covered by AGENT_OPERATIONS_FINAL_REPORT_V2.md.

Planning only: no new paid execution, autonomous write authority, credentials, tool grants, Production capabilities or mandatory lead-model calls. Chief remains GPT-6.1 Sol Medium; independent visual QA also prefers Sol Medium. GPT-4.1 remains an explicit simple-coordination/comparison choice with no automatic paid fallback. Existing five-stage contracts and historical results are preserved.

| Department | Logical lead and future specialists | Boundary and dependencies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technology | Engineering; Website Design; Dashboard Design; Voice & Telephony; Release & Infrastructure | Authorized Preview source and task scope, isolated workspaces/tools, exact-source QA. Engineering cannot approve itself. Release cannot promote Production without separate owner authority. |
| Growth | Marketing; Sales & CRM; Customer Success | Proposed content/analysis first. CRM/customer communications and mutations require separately scoped data permissions and approval policies. No current connector actions granted. |
| Finance & Operations | Finance & Billing; Business Intelligence; Legal & Compliance | Read-only approved aggregates first. Billing actions, legal conclusions and sensitive exports escalate; no model may raise budgets, reinterpret uncertain costs or approve its own permission changes. |
| Quality & Risk | Independent QA; Engineering Auditor / Red Team | Independent evidence access, acceptance/security veto and direct owner escalation. No implementation ownership or authority to waive mandatory failures. Auditor challenges code/contracts/negative paths; QA evaluates outcome/evidence/substance. |

Chief plans, prioritizes, delegates, monitors and reports inside backend-issued scope. Leads initially route capabilities and context as logical roles; they are not extra API invocations. Dedicated lead agents require measured workload need, explicit stage/call/time/cost budgets, tool grants, lease/cancellation and independent acceptance. Quality & Risk is not subordinate to a Chief decision to mark failed work accepted.

The customer-facing AI receptionist is the product runtime serving callers. The internal Voice & Telephony Engineering Agent is a future specialist for implementation/testing of that product: no access to live calls, customer records, telephony control or production credentials is implied by its title. PSTN/acoustic/customer-launch verification requires actual authorized evidence beyond mock/lexical/browser tests.

Implementation sequence: (1) engineering policy and independent review discipline now; (2) durable trusted reviewer attestation and read-only monitoring adapters; (3) verified five-stage read-only completion under separately approved paid pilot; (4) owner-issued graduated grants and isolated Preview engineering executors with transactional reservation/audit/cancellation/rollback; (5) departmental routing/context and only justified dedicated leads. Each increment uses `ENGINEERING_QUALITY.md`; granting a role never grants its tools or budget automatically.

Organizational context must ingest only verified project state, history with limitations, capabilities, active tasks, blockers, budgets and recorded decisions, with provenance/freshness. Keep blind image reviewers free of original conclusions until observations are durably sealed. Backend task state, policy, reservations and independent acceptance remain authoritative. Any critical issue can reach owner escalation without a Chief override. Actual notification delivery is not implemented by this roadmap.

Completed today: review-role policy metadata, risk/plan tooling, acceptance/readiness evaluators and offline anomaly rules. Proposed: authenticated Auditor runtime, durable review receipts, active monitoring/routing, department agents, new write executors and Production capabilities. None of the proposed capabilities is activated.
