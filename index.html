Before you decide

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Want to talk through your setup? Talk to CallerCore →

Can I keep my business number?

Yes. Setup includes reviewing how your existing number can route calls to CallerCore. Your current provider and forwarding options determine the exact steps.

What does the receptionist know?

Your approved services, hours, service area, common questions, and call-handling rules. You can review and update that information in your client portal.

Does signing up turn on live answering?

No. Payment starts setup. Your number, call routing, and receptionist configuration need to be checked before live answering is activated.

Can it book appointments?

CallerCore can capture a caller’s preferred service window. Calendar booking depends on a supported, configured integration; confirm your requirements with us before choosing a plan.

What happens when someone needs me?

You define the transfer and follow-up rules during setup. Requests that still need your attention are kept separate from calls handled without a follow-up.
