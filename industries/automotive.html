CALLERCORE FOR AUTOMOTIVE

Keep the bays moving. Keep callers in the loop.

A busy service desk gets new repair inquiries, vehicle-status questions, estimate approvals, and supplier calls. CallerCore helps collect the right context while your team works on vehicles and serves customers at the counter.

01

Service inquiries start with the vehicle

Capture the make, model, year, reason for calling, and preferred service date. Your advisor gets a request with context and can discuss the next step without repeating every opening question.

02

Status and estimate questions reach an advisor

Ask for the customer’s name, vehicle reference, and question. Pass the message to your team rather than inventing a repair status, price, or completion time.

03

Everyday shop questions get a consistent answer

Use your approved opening hours, location, general service offerings, and drop-off instructions. Route supplier messages and requests to speak with an advisor according to your shop’s preferences.

BUILT AROUND YOUR OFFICE

The information you approve. The handoff you choose.

We configure your shop information, vehicle intake questions, callback preferences, and routing for existing jobs versus new service requests. Choose which messages go to advisors, management, or the general queue. Pricing, diagnostic judgments, parts availability, and work authorization remain with your team.

Give advisors a useful request to return to without pulling them away from every customer or job already in progress.

CallerCore does not diagnose vehicles, approve repair work, quote unapproved prices, or promise completion dates. Service dates are requests until your team confirms them; live repair status requires an available, supported source.
