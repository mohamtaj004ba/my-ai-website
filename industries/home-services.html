Illustrative call flow

A new customer calls while everyone is out

  1. Caller says

    “Can you help with a project at my home?”

  2. CallerCore understands

    A new inquiry that needs your office.

  3. CallerCore acts

    Gathers the service needed, location and callback preference.

  4. Your team sees

    A contact and a concise request to review.

An existing customer wants an update

  1. Caller says

    “Has someone reviewed my estimate?”

  2. CallerCore understands

    An existing inquiry, with a reference to capture.

  3. CallerCore acts

    Records the question and reference for your office. Your team checks the status; live job-status lookup is not part of the current service.

  4. Your team sees

    A follow-up request with the caller’s context.

Someone calls after hours

  1. Caller says

    “Do you cover my area?”

  2. CallerCore understands

    A routine question about your business.

  3. CallerCore acts

    Uses approved service-area information; captures a callback if more help is needed.

  4. Your team sees

    An inquiry with a clear next step for the next working day.

A caller needs expert judgment

  1. Caller says

    “Is this safe to leave until tomorrow?”

  2. CallerCore understands

    A question that needs a qualified person.

  3. CallerCore acts

    Avoids a safety assessment and follows your approved escalation instructions. Emergencies require the appropriate emergency service.

  4. Your team sees

    The caller’s concern and a request for human follow-up. No dispatch promise.
