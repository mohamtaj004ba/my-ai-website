CALLERCORE FOR HOME SERVICES

Stay on the job without losing the next conversation.

A new estimate, an existing job update, a maintenance question, or a supplier delivery can arrive while your team is on site. CallerCore helps distinguish what the caller needs and bring back the details that make a callback productive.

01

New work comes with the essentials

Capture the service requested, location, a brief description, and callback availability. Use your approved service-area information so your team can review whether the request is a fit.

02

Existing jobs do not get lost among new leads

Ask whether the caller is following up on work, an estimate, or a previous visit. Include the relevant name, address, and question so the message reaches the right person.

03

After-hours calls follow your rules

Answer approved business information and collect requests outside office hours. Define which reports need an escalation attempt and which can wait for the normal callback queue.

BUILT AROUND YOUR OFFICE

The information you approve. The handoff you choose.

Set up the trades and services you offer, your service area, approved information, intake questions, and message routing. Distinguish new estimates from warranty questions, maintenance, billing, and existing-job updates. Decide what your team needs to know before calling back and how urgent reports should be passed along.

Stay present with the customer you are helping while the next caller has a clear way to leave a useful request.

CallerCore does not diagnose hazards, guarantee emergency dispatch, or promise a service visit. Callers facing an emergency should contact the appropriate emergency service. Your team confirms prices, availability, and appointments.
