CALLERCORE FOR LEGAL TEAMS

Make the first call easier. Give intake a better starting point.

When attorneys are in a meeting or preparing a matter, the phone still rings. CallerCore helps separate prospective-client inquiries, existing-client messages, and routine office questions so your intake team knows who needs a response and why.

01

New matters, without a rushed intake

Capture the general practice area, a brief reason for calling, contact details, and the best callback time. Your team can start a conversation with context instead of returning an unexplained missed call.

02

Existing clients reach the right team

Ask who the caller is trying to reach and take a concise message. Route messages according to your firm’s instructions, keeping new inquiries distinct from updates on an existing matter.

03

Routine questions stop interrupting focused work

Use approved answers about office hours, location, consultation procedures, and documents to bring. Questions requiring an attorney’s judgment become messages for your team.

BUILT AROUND YOUR OFFICE

The information you approve. The handoff you choose.

We build intake around your practice areas, the information you want collected before a callback, and who receives each type of message. Define what counts as time-sensitive and how those messages should be routed. Your staff keeps control of conflict checks, representation, consultation availability, and advice.

Let attorneys stay with the work in front of them while the intake team receives a useful, organized starting point.

CallerCore does not provide legal advice, perform conflict checks, accept matters, or establish an attorney-client relationship. Collect only the preliminary information your firm approves; your team confirms appointments.
