CALLERCORE FOR MEDICAL PRACTICES

Keep routine office calls from taking over the front desk.

Opening hours, directions, appointment requests, and messages for the office can compete with the person standing at reception. CallerCore can support approved administrative inquiries, with clear boundaries around clinical questions and sensitive information.

01

A clear path for scheduling requests

Capture a request for a new appointment or a change, the caller’s callback details, and preferred availability. The scheduling team confirms the appointment; the receptionist does not promise a slot.

02

Approved office information, consistently

Answer general questions about your location, opening hours, parking, and administrative procedures using information your practice provides. Keep responses consistent when staff are busy.

03

Messages reach the office team

Separate routine administrative messages from requests to speak with staff. Follow the escalation instructions your practice approves rather than interpreting symptoms or assessing clinical urgency.

BUILT AROUND YOUR OFFICE

The information you approve. The handoff you choose.

Start with your permitted administrative use cases, approved office information, and the messages your staff wants to receive. Set explicit instructions for requests outside that scope. Before using CallerCore in a healthcare workflow, discuss your privacy, contractual, and compliance requirements with us.
