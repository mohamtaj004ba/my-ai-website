CALLERCORE FOR PROPERTY

Know which property, which request, and who needs to act.

Tenant maintenance, prospective renters, owners, and contractors all call the same office. CallerCore helps distinguish those conversations and capture the property context your team needs before following up.

01

Maintenance messages with useful context

Ask for the property and unit, a brief description, when the issue began, and callback details. Apply your approved routing instructions so staff can review the request without first chasing the address.

02

Leasing inquiries stay separate

Capture the property of interest, a general move-in timeframe, and preferred contact time. Answer approved listing and office information; your leasing team confirms availability and viewings.

03

Owner and contractor updates reach the right person

Identify whether the caller is an owner, resident, or service provider. Record the property reference and message, then route it to the appropriate team according to your rules.
