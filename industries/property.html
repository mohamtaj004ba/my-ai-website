Resident maintenance

Oak House Management · a resident maintenance report

9 conversation turns
CALLERCORE

Thanks for calling Oak House Management. I’m Maya, the AI receptionist. How can I help?

CALLER

The heating in my apartment stopped working this morning.

CALLERCORE

I can take a report for maintenance. What’s your building address and unit?

CALLER

10 Oak Street, apartment four. I’m Nora Garcia.

CALLERCORE

Thanks, Nora. Is this a new issue, or have you already reported it to the team?

CALLER

New. It was working last night, but the rooms are cold now.

CALLERCORE

I’ll include when it stopped and that it hasn’t been reported yet. What number should maintenance use to contact you?

CALLER

202-555-0140. I’m home all afternoon.

CALLERCORE

I’ve recorded no heating at 10 Oak Street, unit four, first noticed this morning, and your afternoon availability. Maintenance will review the report and contact you about the next step; I haven’t scheduled a repair.
