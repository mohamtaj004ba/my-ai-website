Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: October 5, 2026
Operator: This website and the CallerCore service are operated by CallerCore ("CallerCore," "we," "us," or "our"), based in Spokane, Washington, USA. Contact: support@callercore.com.

This Privacy Policy explains what information CallerCore collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to callercore.com and the CallerCore AI phone-answering and automation service (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information generated through your use of the Service:

  • Contact and business details you submit through forms, our chat assistant, demo interactions, or by contacting us — such as your name, business name, email address, phone number, industry, and the content of your messages.
  • Call and communication data processed by the Service on your behalf when CallerCore answers or handles calls, including caller phone numbers, call recordings or transcripts where enabled, appointment details, and messages.
  • Account and billing information needed to provide the Service and process payments. Payment card details are handled by our payment processor (Stripe) and are not stored by us.
  • Technical and usage data such as pages visited, referring source, approximate location derived from IP address, browser type, and interactions with the site.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the Service, including answering and routing calls, capturing leads, and recording preferred availability.
  • To respond to inquiries, schedule and conduct demos, and provide customer support.
  • To send service-related communications, and — where you have opted in — marketing or follow-up messages. You can opt out at any time.
  • To process payments and manage your account.
  • To maintain security, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

2A. Sensitive and Regulated Data

The standard CallerCore service is not designed for protected health information, full payment-card data, financial-account credentials, government identification numbers, authentication secrets, or other data requiring a separately regulated environment. Please do not submit this information through website forms, onboarding, chat, or call-handling configurations. Businesses with regulated-data requirements should contact support@callercore.com before signup or go-live.

3. AI Processing

Our chat assistant and call-handling features use third-party artificial-intelligence providers to generate responses. Information you submit through these features may be processed by those providers solely to deliver the Service. We do not sell this information.

4. Connected Email Accounts

If an authorized CallerCore administrator connects a Gmail account, CallerCore uses Google OAuth to access only the permissions granted during connection so the administrator can view, organize, and reply to business email from the CallerCore admin dashboard. Gmail messages remain in Google; CallerCore retrieves the message and thread information needed to display and operate the unified inbox. OAuth credentials are stored in encrypted form. Disconnecting Gmail stops CallerCore from using the stored connection, but it does not delete messages from Gmail.

CallerCore does not use connected Gmail content for advertising and does not sell Gmail data. Access is used to provide the connected inbox, message analytics, lead matching, and reply functionality requested by the account administrator.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run the Service:

  • Service providers that operate our infrastructure — including hosting, business operations, voice and messaging, email delivery, AI processing, and payment processing — under agreements that limit their use of the data to providing services to us.
  • Legal and safety disclosures where required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of CallerCore, our customers, or others.
  • Business transfers in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

6. SMS and Messaging Consent

If you provide a phone number and opt in to text messages, you consent to receive service and, where applicable, marketing messages from CallerCore. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for assistance. Consent to messaging is not a condition of purchase. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

7. Cookies and Analytics

Optional website analytics is disabled until you choose Allow analytics. Essential only keeps this measurement disabled. Reopen Cookie settings to change your choice. See our Cookie Policy.

We use first-party browser storage and similar technologies to understand how visitors use callercore.com and to improve our sales and onboarding experience. This may include a pseudonymous visitor identifier and session identifier, pages viewed, active time on pages, navigation and button interactions, form starts or abandonment, plan interest, chatbot activity, referring page, campaign parameters, device category, and approximate city/region/country information supplied by our hosting provider. We do not use this analytics layer to capture passwords, payment-card data, or the contents of ordinary form fields while you type.

When you intentionally submit information through a contact form, signup form, or chatbot handoff, that submitted information may be associated with the website journey so we can respond to the inquiry, understand conversion, and provide support. Payment-card details continue to be handled by Stripe rather than stored by CallerCore.

You can clear first-party site identifiers through your browser's site-data controls. Depending on your location, additional consent or opt-out rights may apply; you may contact support@callercore.com with a privacy request.

8. Data Retention

We use category-specific retention periods designed to keep information only as long as reasonably needed for service delivery, support, security, billing, contractual obligations, and legal requirements. Raw website/session analytics are generally retained for a limited period, while operational records such as calls, messages, leads, support records, and account data are retained for defined periods appropriate to their purpose.

We may retain aggregated or de-identified business analytics indefinitely where the information no longer identifies an individual. This allows CallerCore to measure historical performance, trends, conversion rates, service usage, and year-over-year progress without retaining unnecessary raw personal data.

Account cancellation does not automatically mean immediate deletion of all records. Customers may request deletion by contacting support@callercore.com. Certain records may be retained for limited periods where needed for billing, contracts, security, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or opt-out suppression. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Choices and Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may opt out of marketing communications. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable law. To make a request, email support@callercore.com.

11. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above and, where appropriate, by additional notice.

13. Contact Us

Questions about this Policy or your information can be sent to support@callercore.com.

Detailed information

How information enters CallerCore

Information can reach us in different ways. A visitor may submit a contact or signup form, send a message through the website assistant, or contact support. A customer may enter its business information, approved answers, routing preferences, and intake questions. A caller may provide a name, callback number, address, reason for calling, or preferred availability during a conversation. These different records have different purposes; a website visit is not the same as an authenticated customer record.

When you use a form or assistant, provide only the information needed for the request. Information intentionally submitted for a sales or support inquiry can be used to respond even if optional website analytics is disabled. Choosing Essential only does not prevent contact forms, requested assistance, or necessary security processing.

Website measurement and your choice

Optional website measurement uses first-party identifiers to connect visits and events such as page views, selected plans, link clicks, active page time, and form starts. It does not record ordinary form-field values as you type. Submitted inquiries are handled separately from passive measurement. The cookie prompt allows you to permit analytics or continue with essential storage only.

Analytics identifiers are not created by this optional measurement layer before permission is given. You can change your choice through Cookie settings. Withdrawing permission stops subsequent optional measurement and removes the first-party visitor and session identifiers from that browser; it does not automatically erase records already received by our servers. Contact us if you also want to request deletion of historical information. See the Cookie Policy for storage details.

Business workspaces and access

Account information helps identify the business, its plan, and the person accessing the workspace. Dashboard records can include call outcomes, captured requests, contact histories, team notes, and follow-up status. These records help the customer review what happened and decide what action to take. Access depends on the account and the permissions available to it.

Customers should limit access to people who need the information for their role, keep their credentials private, and notify us about suspected unauthorized access. CallerCore staff may access relevant records to provide support, investigate problems, operate the service, or meet legal obligations. A public example dashboard uses fictional sample data and does not expose an actual customer workspace.

Call records, recordings, and transcripts

Where call-handling features are enabled, the service may process caller information, conversation content, call timing, duration, classification, and outcomes. Recording and transcription depend on the configured service and available provider capabilities. A transcript or AI summary may contain errors or omit context. Businesses should review important details before relying on them.

Customers are responsible for deciding what their receptionist should ask, what information it may disclose, and how calls should be routed. Customers must arrange any notices or permissions required for recording, transcription, or other communication processing. Do not place secrets, full card numbers, or regulated sensitive information into greetings, business knowledge, intake questions, or ordinary support messages.

Our role and the customer’s role

For business account administration, website inquiries, service support, and our own operational needs, CallerCore determines how relevant information is used. When we process call records or customer-provided business information to deliver configured services, we act on behalf of the business customer within the service relationship. The precise allocation of responsibilities can depend on the applicable agreement and law.

If you called a business using CallerCore and want to correct or delete information held by that business, you may contact that business directly. We can help identify and route an appropriate request, but may need instructions from the business before changing its service records. We will also respond to requests concerning information we maintain for our own purposes.

Providers, connections, and external sites

Service delivery may involve hosting, storage, AI, voice, email, and payment providers. We disclose information necessary for the relevant feature or operation, rather than treating a connection as permission for unrelated uses. A customer-selected integration can involve additional information exchange according to its configuration. External providers may maintain their own records under their policies.

Links to another website, a payment page, or a connected service do not make that service part of this website’s privacy controls. Review the destination’s notices and settings. Disconnecting an integration prevents further use of that saved connection where supported, but does not recall information already sent or delete records maintained independently by the destination.

Retention, account closure, and deletion requests

The time we keep information depends on why it was collected, the customer relationship, operational requirements, and applicable obligations. Account closure, subscription cancellation, and a privacy deletion request are different actions. Cancelling a subscription does not by itself identify every record that must be deleted, and it may leave information needed for invoices, support history, disputes, security, or legal obligations.

A deletion request should identify the account or contact information involved and the categories you want us to review. We may need to verify the requester and clarify the scope before acting. Deletion can affect the availability of historical records. Some information may remain for an applicable exception or in backup systems until those systems complete their normal lifecycle; we do not promise immediate removal from every copy.

Making a privacy request

To ask for access, correction, deletion, or an explanation of how your information is used, email support@callercore.com. Describe the request and provide a way to locate the relevant account or interaction. Do not send identity documents, passwords, or other sensitive verification material unless we request an appropriate method.

We may ask for reasonable verification to avoid disclosing or changing someone else’s records. Depending on the law that applies, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, obtain a portable copy, withdraw permission, or complain to an appropriate supervisory authority. We assess requests under the applicable rules and explain relevant limitations. We do not claim that every jurisdiction provides identical rights or deadlines.

Cross-border processing and service locations

CallerCore is based in the United States, and information may be processed where our service providers operate. Those locations can have different privacy laws from your location. The safeguards and contractual arrangements applicable to a particular service depend on the relevant provider and agreement. Contact us before onboarding if your organization requires a particular processing location or transfer arrangement.

We do not represent that the standard service offers a particular data-residency arrangement or a regulated compliance configuration. Businesses with healthcare, legal, financial, or other sensitive-data obligations should establish the requirements for their intended use before sending that information to the service.

Changes, questions, and related policies

We may revise this notice as our features and information practices develop. The review date identifies the current version displayed on this page. Where appropriate, we provide additional notice of a material change. A new description does not authorize a use that requires additional permission or another legal basis.

Read this notice together with the Terms of Use and Cookie Policy. For questions about a feature, a provider, a request, or an information practice that is not clear here, contact support@callercore.com before providing information you are unsure about.
